# Shipping and delivery

Standard shipping normally arrives within 3–7 business days after an order ships. Processing time before shipment is usually 1–2 business days.

Tracking becomes available after the carrier scans the package. Carrier delays, weather, and remote destinations can affect the estimated delivery date.

If tracking has not updated for 3 business days or an order is marked delivered but cannot be located, contact support so the delivery can be investigated.
