# Returns and refunds

SupportPilot AI accepts returns of unused items in their original condition within 30 calendar days of delivery. A receipt or order number is required.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and inspected. Most approved refunds appear within 5–10 business days, depending on the payment provider.

Final-sale items and personalized products are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or defective. For damaged items, contact support within 7 days of delivery and keep the packaging while the claim is reviewed.
